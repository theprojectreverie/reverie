# Royal Gardens — Curated Design Review

This is a curated, non-destructive selection from the incoming 809 extracted crops.
It is a visual-design review library, not an approved runtime tileset.

## Visual direction
- Maintain a coherent rose-lavender stone palette with restrained antique-gold accents.
- Use the supplied gate/castle silhouettes as landmark dressing, not as a repeating wall texture.
- Build the playable route from connected paving modules; verify every straight, corner, T, cross, edge and transition in the editor.
- Use hedge and flower-border pieces to frame routes without narrowing the walkable corridor.
- Keep fountains, benches, statues, lamps, bridges and gazebos as multi-tile props with intentional footprints.
- The asset crops are soft, painterly and variably sized. Do not force every object into 32x32 or treat them as a consistent pixel-art tileset.
- No sprite is approved until its alpha edge, source silhouette, dimensions, pivot, scale and visual style have been checked individually.
